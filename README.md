# EctoClean Academy

**Clean Nutrition + Home Training for Ectomorphs**  
A science-based mobile app designed for adolescents (especially 15-year-old ectomorphs) who want to gain lean muscle, improve skin health, and follow a complete daily routine — all without ultra-processed foods that cause acne.

---

## Overview

EctoClean Academy is a complete lifestyle app that combines:

- Clean, anti-acne nutrition
- Evidence-based home workouts
- Smart shopping list with price calculation
- Advanced nutrition calculator (by grams/kg)
- Permanent daily food diary
- Real-time Brasília clock & date
- Progress tracking

Built specifically for ectomorphs who train at home and want real, sustainable results.

---

## Main Features

### 1. Daily Routine
- Automated daily schedule (Breakfast, Snacks, Lunch, Workout, Dinner)
- Editable times
- Clean meal suggestions every day
- Anti-acne food focus (non-comedogenic, non-ultra-processed)

### 2. Nutrition System
- **Nutrition Calculator**: Enter any food + weight in grams or kilograms → full nutritional breakdown (calories, protein, carbs, fats, fiber, vitamins, etc.)
- Complete nutritional tables scaled to the exact weight entered
- Smart food suggestions (only clean foods)

### 3. Permanent Food Diary
- Free-text registration of everything you eat
- Confirmation of quantity + meal type
- Permanent history (never deleted)
- Daily totals (calories, macros)
- Calendar view of past days
- Export to PDF

### 4. Smart Shopping List
- Auto-generated from meal plan
- Product name + quantity + image + estimated price (R$)
- Real-time total calculation
- Mark as purchased

### 5. Home Training System
- Designed for 15-year-old ectomorph (51 kg)
- 4–5 training days + rest days
- Full muscle coverage (upper body, core, lower body + stabilizers)
- Progressive overload over 4 weeks
- Bodyweight + minimal equipment (chair, water bottles, backpack)
- Clear sets, reps, rest times and alternatives

### 6. Extra Features
- Real-time Brasília clock (day, month, year)
- Progress tracking (weight, measurements, photos, strength)
- Dark / Light mode
- Offline support
- Educational tips (“Why this food doesn’t cause acne”, “Why this works for ectomorphs”)
